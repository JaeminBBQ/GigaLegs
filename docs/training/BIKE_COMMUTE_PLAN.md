# Bike Commute Plan: Five Ridges → South Meadows

## The route (estimates — verify, see `handoffs/HUMAN.md`)
- **Start:** Five Ridges, north Sparks (off Pyramid Way / Sparks Blvd area).
- **Destination:** work in South Meadows (exact address is in `athlete/PROFILE.md`, which is private).
- **Path:** south on Sparks Blvd → onto Veterans Pkwy → south to South Meadows.
- **Distance:** estimated **~17–19 mi one way** (~35 mi round trip), not measured. To measure it: in Google Maps, get directions from home to work, pick the bike icon, choose the Sparks Blvd / Veterans Pkwy option, and record the miles and the elevation gain shown under the route.
- **Elevation:** Five Ridges sits noticeably higher than the valley floor, so the **morning ride in is mostly downhill and the ride home is a net climb** (likely a few hundred feet). Truckee Meadows afternoon winds are often from the west/southwest — the ride home is the hard leg.
- **Safety recon to do on a weekend, by bike, before the first commute:** the I-80 crossing on Sparks Blvd, intersections and any gaps in bike lanes along Sparks Blvd / Veterans Pkwy, and where you'll lock up/change at work. Ride with lights front and rear (daylight too; mandatory once it gets dark early in winter).

## Progression stages
Advance a stage when you've completed its target for **2 consecutive weeks** without it hurting your lifting (readiness soreness ≤ 5, lifting RPE on target). All riding is Zone 1–2 unless stated.

| Stage | Weekly target | Longest ride | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Base** (during on-ramp) | 2–3 rides, 60–120 min total | 45 min | Flat and easy: Sparks Marina, Truckee River path. Get bike fit/saddle comfortable. |
| **2. Build** | 3 rides, 2–3 h total | 60–75 min (~12–15 mi) | Include some of the real route. Practice the climb home at easy effort. |
| **3. Recon + one-way** | 1 one-way commute (downhill morning ride in) + 2 easy rides | One-way commute | Get home by RTC bus (bike rack), car, or ride the next morning. Full route recon done on a weekend first. |
| **4. First round trips** | 1 round trip + 1 one-way | Round trip | Ride home is Zone 2 — it's long; go slow. Eat before the ride home. |
| **5. Regular commuter** | 2–3 round trips | Round trip | Stay here. Cut to ≤ 2 round trips/week in the last 2–3 weeks of the deadlift program (peak). |

Rough timeline: Stage 5 in ~10–14 weeks if nothing stalls. Weekly ride time should grow by roughly ≤ 15–20%.

## Interaction with lifting (summary — full rules in METHODOLOGY §5)
- Commute days ideally = non-lifting days.
- No hard riding the day before heavy squat/deadlift.
- If leg soreness ≥ 7, swap a commute for the bus/car — no penalty to streaks.
- Fuel the ride: a round trip of this length is a big energy cost. Under-eating will stall squat and deadlift progress.

## Bike basics (you have a geared bike; this is all you need to know to start)
- **Get a tune-up first.** Before Stage 2, take the bike to a local shop and ask for a basic tune-up and a saddle-height check. Tell them you're going to commute about 35 mi round trip, and ask whether your tires suit road riding.
- **Gears:** the left shifter changes the front rings (big jumps), and the right shifter changes the rear cogs (small steps).
  - Climbing / the ride home: small front ring + bigger rear cogs = easy pedaling.
  - Flats and downhill: bigger front ring + smaller rear cogs.
  - Shift *before* a hill, while still pedaling but easing off the pressure. Never shift under heavy force.
  - Avoid "cross-chaining" (big front + biggest rear, or small front + smallest rear).
  - Aim for a smooth ~75–90 pedal revolutions per minute. Spinning easy gears saves your legs for squats; grinding hard gears doesn't.
- **Saddle height:** with your heel on the pedal at the bottom of the stroke, your leg is almost straight. In normal pedaling, your knee stays slightly bent at the bottom.
- **Weekly 2-minute check:** tire pressure (the range is printed on the tire sidewall), brakes grab firmly, chain isn't dry or squeaky (lube it every ~100–150 mi).
- **Learn to fix a flat** once at home before you rely on the bike for commuting. A YouTube video and one practice run is enough.

## Kit checklist
Helmet, front + rear lights, lock, flat kit (tube, levers, mini pump/CO2), water, a snack for the ride home, layers (Reno mornings are cold Oct–Apr), change of clothes or a plan to change at work.

# Nutrition

Bodyweight: see `athlete/PROFILE.md` (private). The gram targets below are computed from the 2026-10-03 bodyweight; recompute if it changes by more than ~10 lb. Goal: maintain during the on-ramp + deadlift program, then lose a few pounds slowly afterwards (D9). We only set targets here; the app doesn't track food (non-goal).

## Daily targets
| | Target | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Protein | **140–160 g** | Program floor is 1.6 g/kg ≈ 131 g; a little higher to support the later cut |
| Fat | **≥ 55 g** (typically 60–80 g) | Program floor 0.65 g/kg ≈ 53 g |
| Carbs | The rest | More on lifting and commute days |
| Calories | **Maintenance**, found from your scale trend (below) | |

## Finding maintenance (no calorie math needed)
1. Weigh in each morning after the bathroom, before eating. Log it (the app will have a field for this; until then, use a note).
2. Look at the **7-day average**, not single days. Day-to-day swings of 1–3 lb are water.
3. During the program, if the weekly average drops more than ~1 lb below your starting weight for 2 weeks in a row, add ~200–300 kcal/day (e.g. a bigger lunch, a shake). If it climbs more than ~2 lb, trim a little.

## Ride days
A round-trip commute of ~35 mi at an easy pace burns very roughly 1,000+ kcal on top of a normal day. On commute days:
- Eat a real breakfast before riding in, and a carb-containing snack before the ride home (the uphill, often into the wind).
- Add roughly one extra meal's worth of food across the day.
- For rides over 90 minutes, bring water and a snack.

## After the program: the cut
During the deload + squat volume block: aim for ~0.5–1 lb/week down on the 7-day average (≈250–500 kcal/day below maintenance). Keep protein at the top of the range. If squat performance drops two weeks in a row, slow the cut down.

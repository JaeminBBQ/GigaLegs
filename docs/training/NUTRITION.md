# Nutrition (v2, D31)

Bodyweight: see `athlete/PROFILE.md` (private). The gram targets below are computed from the 2026-10 bodyweight; recompute if it changes by more than ~10 lb. Goal: **get leaner slowly while keeping muscle and training quality** through the season. We only set targets here; the app doesn't track food (non-goal).

## Daily targets
| | Target | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Protein | **160–180 g** | ~0.9–1 g per lb: keeps muscle while in a small deficit |
| Fat | **≥ 55 g** (typically 60–80 g) | ~0.65 g/kg floor |
| Carbs | The rest | More on long-run, long-ride, and gym days |
| Calories | **~250–400 kcal below maintenance** most weeks (see exceptions) | Aim for 0.25–0.75 lb/week down |

## Running the deficit by the scale (no calorie math needed)
1. Weigh in each morning after the bathroom, before eating, and log it.
2. Watch the **7-day average**, not single days (1–3 lb daily swings are water).
3. Losing **< 0.25 lb/week** for 3 weeks → trim ~200 kcal/day. Losing **> 1 lb/week** → add ~200 kcal/day.
4. **Gym weights or long runs going backwards 2 weeks in a row** → eat at maintenance for 2 weeks, then resume.

## Exceptions: eat at maintenance or above
- Race weeks (Weeks 26 and 32) and the 2–3 days before each event: carb up a bit.
- Down weeks are fine to keep the deficit; sickness is not.
- Days with a long run > 75 min, a long ride > 2 h, or a round-trip commute: eat back most of it (a round-trip commute is very roughly 1,000+ kcal). Don't diet on those days; diet on the quiet ones.

## Around sessions
- Long run > 75 min: water, and from ~10 mi a gel or chews every ~40 min (practice in Weeks 21–23 what you'll use on race day).
- Rides > 90 min: a bottle per hour and 30–60 g carbs per hour from the first hour.
- Protein in the meal after the gym (any normal meal is fine; the "anabolic window" is wide).

# Season Plan 2026–27 (D28, D32)

Rules are in `METHODOLOGY.md`; this file is the calendar. Week 1 starts **Mon Oct 26, 2026**. Weeks are checklists (METHODOLOGY §3): the dates show where a week *should* land; if one stretches, later weeks shift and Claude re-checks the race-week math at the weekly check-in.

**Hybrid all season (D32):** running and riding build together from Week 1. Every normal week has 3 runs, **2 rides** (one easy, one long), and 3 gym sessions. The ride volume is where most of the aerobic building happens early, because cycling is low-impact and can grow faster than running safely can.

**Winter riding:** the gym bike or a home trainer counts for any ride. Long rides go outside on dry, daylight days; if the weather won't allow it, ride what you can indoors (up to ~90 min) and that week still counts. Never ride on ice.

## Starter week (Oct 20–25): just build the habit
- Do **something** every day (any floor option counts).
- Try **Gym A once and Gym B once** at RPE ≤ 7, to choose your exercise options and find starting weights.
- One easy 10-min jog. If it feels trivially easy, good: Week 1 stays as written anyway.
- One easy ride, 30–45 min.
- Get running shoes fitted. Bike: check tires and brakes.

## Phase 1: Easy-jog base (Weeks 1–8, Oct 26 – Dec 20)

## Week 26: Half marathon (Apr 19–24)
Two short easy runs (20–25 min, one with 4 strides) and one easy 30–45 min spin early in the week, 1 upper-body gym session by Wednesday, floors otherwise. **Race Saturday:** start slower than feels right for the first 3 miles; walk the water stations if you like. Goal: finish strong and healthy.

## Phase 4: Ride peak (Weeks 27–32, Apr 26 – Jun 6)
Runs drop to 2 easy/week (keeps the base for the fall marathon). Gym back to 3/week (Gym A legs: cap RPE 7 the day before a long ride). Rides are easy unless noted; climbs at a steady, sustainable effort.

Event fueling (practice it on every long ride over 2 h): 30–60 g carbs per hour from the first hour, a bottle per hour, more in heat. Tahoe sits around 6,200 ft, a bit higher than home; just keep the effort conversational on climbs.
